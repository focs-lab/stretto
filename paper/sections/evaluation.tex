\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\pending{Every number in this section is provisional (blue): it comes from the run before
the soundness and evidence fixes, and the tables are regenerated from the CSV files by
\texttt{experiments/tables.py} after the rerun.}

We ask six questions.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=*,style=nextline]
\item[RQ1, soundness in practice.] Does the check ever accept an alternative that no
  execution realises, and does every replayed finding behave as predicted?
\item[RQ2, effectiveness.] Which labelled bugs does \tool find from one run, compared
  with \MUST on the same programs and with repeated randomised runs?
\item[RQ3, buffering.] How many recorded runs and alternatives depend on the threshold,
  and do the predicted thresholds hold on a real implementation?
\item[RQ4, levels.] Where do \Lone, \LoneSP and \Ltwo disagree, and why?
\item[RQ5, cost.] What do tracing and analysis cost?
\item[RQ6, real applications.] What does \tool report on wildcard-heavy correct code, and
  what do its alternatives change?
\end{description}

\paragraph{Setup.} Programs are built with Open MPI~4.1.6 and MPICH~4.2 on Ubuntu~24.04
and run on one node. Corpora: the message-race, tag-matching, buffering and probe programs
of the MPI Bugs Initiative~\citep{laurent21mbi} (\nMBIprogs programs, \nMBIcorrect of
them correct), and the point-to-point and collective programs of \MBB
level~2~\citep{jammer24mbb} (\nMBBprogs). The baseline is \MUST~1.9.2, run in \MBB's
container with the same timeout. \pending{Stress-testing baseline: $N$ runs per program
with random delays injected by the replay library. PARCOACH if it builds from the
\MBB container.}

\subsection{RQ1: soundness in practice}

\pending{E6 rerun on the fixed checker. The comparison is against an exhaustive explorer
of $\SP(\trace)$, over every judged pair rather than only matched ones, on corpus and idiom
traces and on random programs extended with synchronous and ready sends, probes,
\mpicall{Waitany} and sends after receives. Before the fix: \nEsixPairs (alternative,
threshold) pairs, \nEsixUnsound unsound, \nEsixIncomplete incomplete, at most
\nEsixMaxEvents events per trace. The audit's two counterexamples were outside the
generator's reach.}

\pending{Replays: findings, distinct pinned runs and programs, each with match logging
and a control. Before the fix: \nSPfindings \LoneSP findings, \nReplayRuns distinct
pinned runs in \nReplayPrograms programs, all of which hung as predicted.}

\subsection{RQ3: buffering}

\pending{E2 over \nEtwoTraces traces: \nEtwoObsBuf recorded runs realisable only above a
threshold; alternatives that need a threshold; Open MPI's eager limit measured at
\BimplOMPI bytes by default and \BminOMPI at the smallest forced setting. The replay with
the limit forced down is the confirmation. Idiom 03 is the case it can test. The two
persistent-send replays that hung where the prediction says they finish must be explained
first.}

\subsection{RQ4: levels}

\pending{E3 and E7: the single-path deadlock of the master--worker idiom that the real
program cannot reach; MBI's payload-guarded races certified by twins as \cls{1e}; \Lone
deadlocks (\cref{thm:l1dead}) where they exist.}

\subsection{RQ5: cost}

\pending{E4 repeated five times with NPB's own timers: the traced run takes
\TraceOverhead times the plain one. Analysis: \nAMGalts probe alternatives of AMG in
\AMGtime\,s. Nekbone's communication suite, \NekRecords million records and
\nNekAlts receive alternatives, in \NekMinutes minutes and \NekGB GB on a shared host,
which is an upper bound.}

\subsection{RQ6: real applications}

\pending{AMG (probe-driven exchange) and Nekbone (reduction tree): \Lone deadlocks searched
by \cref{thm:l1dead}; 30 alternatives each replayed with controls; findings grouped per
call site. Nekbone's wildcard reduction tree (\texttt{gp2}) belongs to the communication
suite's timing test; the solver sums with \mpicall{MPI\_Allreduce}, so the planned
numerical-irreproducibility experiment needs another code. The HYPRE~2.10.1 nondeterminism,
if one traced run predicts it.}

\subsection{Threats to validity}

\pending{One node and few ranks; the corpora's programs are small; twins are written
knowing the labels; \MUST runs a different build of each program under MPICH, so it sees
a different run; the model's premises (complete trace of the modelled fragment, a single
threshold per implementation, angelic \mpicall{Waitany}).}
