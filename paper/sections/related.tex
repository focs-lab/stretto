\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Predictive analysis for shared memory, and its origin.} Feasible program
prefixes~\citep{netzer91accuracy} already excluded every event that a changed event might
control. The maximal causal model~\citep{serbanuta12mcm} made the program model explicit: a
thread is deterministic given what it reads, every program that can produce the trace is
considered, and a thread stops at its first read whose value differs; the resulting set of
runs is sound and maximal. RVPredict~\citep{huang14rvpredict} and
SeqCheck~\citep{cai21seqcheck} let such a thread run on to its next branch, and symbolic
predictive analysis keeps the recorded branch conditions~\citep{wang09ctp}. Correct
reorderings~\citep{smaragdakis12cp} keep every read's writer. WCP~\citep{kini17wcp} weakened
happens-before soundly in linear time, and SHB~\citep{mathur18shb} made it sound beyond the
first race, with relaxed correct reorderings that let a thread's last read change. M2~\citep{pavlogiannis20m2} and
SyncP~\citep{mathur21syncp} decide larger classes by closure, SyncP optimally for the
reorderings that keep the order of critical sections. Sync-preserving deadlock
prediction runs in linear time~\citep{tunc23spd} and reports gaps in an SMT encoding claimed
sound~\citep{kalhauge18sound}. Synchronisation-reversal races~\citep{shi24osr} reverse one lock
order and test acyclicity over a closure, the shape of our check; reversing one match is the
message-passing analogue. \Cref{thm:sound} follows the unified soundness
template~\citep{liu25soundness}, with MPI's rules at $\Bt$ for the memory model and a matching
over the horizon for the bug; the template covers locks under multicopy-atomic models, not
message passing. Our witness is uniform over the programs consistent on $\Hpre$.

\paragraph{Message races and reachability testing.} Race analysis of
traces~\citep{tai97race} and reachability testing~\citep{lei06reachability} compute from one
message-passing trace the race set of each receive: the sends not causally after
it that are the earliest unconsumed message of their source, as our steals are. A race variant
changes receives to members of their race sets and removes what a changed receive may
control, which for blocking traffic matches our horizon. Removing it makes
a variant feasible ``regardless of the program's control and data
flow''~\citep[p.\,390]{lei06reachability}.
The line's first paper proves feasibility for the read--write sequences of shared
memory~\citep{hwang94apsec}; for message passing we found it asserted, not proved. The model has no
nonblocking receives, probes, collectives or buffering threshold, and happens-before
concurrency suffices there; for MPI it does not (\cref{sec:graph}). Trace race detection covers
nonblocking operations~\citep{cypher95race} and first and unaffected
races~\citep{netzer96msgrace,park04unaffected,park07mpirace}. The MAD
environment computed the racing messages of MPI programs with the same one-per-source bound,
cut the trace at the causal future of an exchanged message, and replayed the rest with
other wildcards pinned~\citep{kranzlmueller00thesis,kranzlmueller00mad}. For MPI's semantics,
\tool proves the feasibility these works assert or test.

\paragraph{Prediction for MPI\@.} Trace-based prediction of alternative matchings began with SMT
encodings for MCAPI that search the arrival orders of a trace for assertion
violations~\citep{elwakil10padtad,huang13ase}, a problem shown NP-complete~\citep{huang13ase},
and with a predictor of orphaning deadlocks from one PMPI trace~\citep{sharma12maaped}.
MOPPER~\citep{forejt14mopper,forejt17mopper} encodes deadlock detection propositionally. Its
line continues with Hermes, which records path conditions over branches on received data,
re-executes under ISP to force other matches, and models bounded
buffers~\citep{khanna18hermes}, and with SIMIAN~\citep{ranjan22simian}. Another line settles
match pairs by an abstract machine and SMT, for deadlocks, message races and zero-buffer
compatibility~\citep{huang20matchpair,huang20ase,huang23tpds}, and a third refines match sets
with logical clocks~\citep{cluster22matchsets,li24parco,internetware24}. MOPPER, the ASE 2020
analysis~\citep{huang20ase} and Hermes state soundness and completeness relative to
single-path programs, or to recorded path conditions, under fixed buffering models. None
certifies an alternative for every program consistent with the trace or computes its
threshold profile. MPI-PRV~\citep{yao26mpiprv} carries pattern-language
monitoring~\citep{ang24pattern} to temporal properties of MPI traces without a solver; it
keeps every wildcard match and assumes zero buffering (\cref{app:mpiprv}).

\paragraph{Exhaustive exploration.} ISP~\citep{vakkalanka08isp} explores the matchings of a
program on one input, rewriting wildcard receives into specific ones during
replay~\citep{gopalakrishnan11cacm}. DAMPI~\citep{vo10dampi} takes from one run the sends not
causally after a wildcard receive, subject to non-overtaking, replays each choice, and reaches
thousands of processes by giving up completeness. MPI-SV~\citep{yu20mpisv} and
CIVL~\citep{siegel15civl} verify symbolically. We re-execute only to confirm.
The stress baseline of \cref{sec:eval} follows
randomised concurrency testing~\citep{wolff24rff,zhao25surw}.

\paragraph{Complexity of message-passing consistency.} Our check is the VCh-rf problem
of \citet{shi26popl} for MPI's channels. Their hardness comes from channels with several
producers and from count capacities; an MPI queue has one producer per (source, destination,
communicator) and blocking is a
per-message threshold, so given the whole matching every constraint is a conjunction and the check
an acyclicity test. In shared memory keeping a read's writer is a disjunction, and predicting a
race that needs one reversed critical section is W[1]-hard~\citep{mathur21syncp}.

\paragraph{Buffering.} Slack elasticity holds for deterministic programs~\citep{manohar98slack}.
With wildcards, adding buffering can add MPI deadlocks and neither zero nor infinite buffering
exposes all~\citep{vakkalanka10slack}; without them, zero buffering
suffices~\citep{siegel05ppopp,siegel07europvm}. Aislinn verifies programs under the threshold we
model~\citep{bohm16aislinn}.

\paragraph{Message passing beyond MPI\@.} Go trace analyses report alternative communications
under a straight-line model~\citep{sulzmann17hvc} or by vector clocks~\citep{sulzmann18go}, and
AdvocateGo replays partners found by happens-before~\citep{advocatego}. Erlang's race
variants~\citep{vidal22racevariants} are our steal and horizon without blocking, with a theorem on
non-redundancy;
Bita~\citep{tasharofi13bita}, DCatch~\citep{liu17dcatch} and dl$^2$~\citep{gao25dl2} force,
trigger or observe such bugs. None proves an alternative feasible.

\paragraph{Confirmation by re-execution.} In shared memory, predictions are confirmed by
controlled scheduling~\citep{bensalem06confirmation,joshi09deadlockfuzzer}, directed random
testing~\citep{sen08racefuzzer}, exact replay of the observed prefix~\citep{sorrentino10penelope}
or re-execution~\citep{farzan12exceptionull,huang15mcr}. In message passing, reachability
testing and MAD replay a prefix with the other wildcards pinned, ISP and DAMPI rewrite wildcard
receives into specific ones~\citep{gopalakrishnan11cacm}, MOPPER can rerun a predicted matching
under ISP~\citep{forejt17mopper}, and MPIWiz~\citep{xue09mpiwiz} and record and
replay~\citep{netzer92replay,sato15rempi} reproduce recorded sources. Specific to \tool are pins
over a horizon that follows rendezvous and ordering dependences, a log of each
pinned match, a control replay that must finish, and confirmation per threshold.

\paragraph{Runtime checkers and benchmarks.} \MUST~\citep{hilbrich12must} checks the observed
run online for argument, resource and type errors and for deadlocks. It treats standard sends
as unbuffered and, when a run stalls at a wildcard receive, tries its other matching sends.
Since version 1.10 it checks MPI buffers through
ThreadSanitizer~\citep{jenke23tsanfibers}. PARCOACH~\citep{saillard14parcoach} checks
collective ordering. NINJA~\citep{sato17ninja} injects network
noise to expose message races and reproduced the HYPRE race we predict;
\cref{sec:eval} compares \tool with \MUST and NINJA.
