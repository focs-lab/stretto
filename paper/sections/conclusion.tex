\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Limitations.} The model covers point-to-point communication, probes,
completion calls and collectives by shape. RMA, neighbourhood collectives, partitioned
communication and memory accesses are outside it. The optional guard reports buffer reuse
during a nonblocking operation when the run does it.
Under \texttt{MPI\_THREAD\_MULTIPLE} we report observed findings only. Consequences beyond
\Lone come from the single-path explorer on small traces, replays, or hand-written twins.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} One recorded run of an MPI program determines more than the run itself.
Over the horizon, the part of the trace every consistent program repeats, an alternative
matching is feasible iff a constraint graph with match nodes is acyclic. Each certificate
carries its buffering thresholds, a level for its consequence, and a pin that forces it. On
MBI, \tool finds \nMBItpunion of \nMBIerrors labelled errors with no false error report, here
and in \nRepsRuns repeated runs. From one clean run, forced certified alternatives expose a
production race in HYPRE and documented bugs in NPB MG and deal.II.
