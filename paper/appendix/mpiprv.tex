\section{Comparison with MPI-PRV}
\label{app:mpiprv}

MPI-PRV~\citep{yao26mpiprv} records six calls (\mpicall{Send}, \mpicall{Recv}, \mpicall{Isend},
\mpicall{Irecv}, \mpicall{Wait} and \mpicall{Barrier}), and buffer accesses as calls to marker
functions placed in the source. It checks a pattern of events against the reorderings of the
trace that its dependences allow, with every wildcard match fixed. We ran its artifact image
unchanged, pinned by digest, with its own scripts (\texttt{experiments/mpiprv/}).

\paragraph{Its evaluation.} Its RQ1 reproduces: each of its \nPRVrqOneProps properties is
reported in each of \nPRVrqOneRounds rounds. On the traces shipped for its \nPRVrqTwoConfigs
application configurations, the analyser reports its pattern in \nPRVrqTwoMatched; in
\nPRVrqTwoAbsent of them an event that the pattern names never occurs in the trace.

\paragraph{Correct programs.} The trace records a receive's source argument, not the sender it
matched. On a correct program whose receive names \texttt{MPI\_ANY\_SOURCE} and then reads its
buffer, MPI-PRV reported the read before the only sender's send, an order no execution has, in
\nPRVprobeWildRecvAny of \nPRVprobeWildRecvAnyRuns runs (\nPRVprobeWildIrecvAny of
\nPRVprobeWildIrecvAnyRuns with \mpicall{Irecv}); with the sender named, in
\nPRVprobeWildRecvSrc of \nPRVprobeWildRecvSrcRuns. Two events on either side of a barrier were
reported in the impossible order in \nPRVprobeOrderBarrierBa of \nPRVprobeOrderBarrierBaRuns
runs, and in \nPRVprobeOrderSendrecvBa of \nPRVprobeOrderSendrecvBaRuns with a send and a
receive in place of the barrier. Its patterns do not relate their events to each other (the
same rank, the same message), so a correct program with four ranks was reported from unrelated
events in \nPRVprobeUnrelated of \nPRVprobeUnrelatedRuns runs.

\paragraph{The Adept kernel.} In its motivating example, a stencil kernel, MPI-PRV reports a
receive buffer used before the receive completes. The interior loop reads planes that the
receives do not write. With the kernel's missing waits added (below), delaying one neighbour's
sends until after the interior loop, all \nPRVadeptDelayOneLate of them, leaves the result
bit-identical to that of the blocking variant, whereas a mutant that updates the halo planes
before the waits changes it. The kernel's actual fault lies elsewhere: it never completes its
halo sends and then overwrites their buffers. The buffer guard reports \nPRVadeptGuard such
writes, \nPRVadeptOrigSame of \nPRVadeptOrigRuns runs of the shipped kernel give the blocking
variant's result, and with the two missing waits \nPRVadeptFixedSame of \nPRVadeptFixedRuns do
and the guard reports \nPRVadeptGuardFixed.

\paragraph{Its patterns on our corpora.} We ran its shipped properties on the \nPRVinScope
programs of MBI and \MBB in its scope: those whose events are all MPI calls unchanged, and the
buffer properties with markers inserted by a script before every access to a buffer that a
nonblocking operation may own. On the local-concurrency errors and the correct programs, the
buffer properties report \nPRVtpBLC errors and \nPRVfpBLC correct programs (F1 \PRVfoneBLC); the
buffer guard reports \nPRVtpStrettoLC and \nPRVfpStrettoLC (F1 \PRVfoneStrettoLC), its false
reports being the \texttt{MPI\_IN\_PLACE} programs of \cref{sec:eval}. The properties on MPI calls
alone report \nPRVfpA correct programs, mostly because send modes outside the six recorded calls
leave receives unmatched in the trace.

\paragraph{\tool on its applications.} We traced its \nPRVapps applications in its image, in
\nPRVappConfigs configurations of up to 24 processes. \tool predicts a deadlock in Heat, where a
receive on any source and any tag can take a message of another tag; replays with the pins of
its horizon hang, and their controls finish. In DTG it predicts a deadlock without buffering,
which a replay with rendezvous forced confirms. In its copies of diffusion2d and poisson, which
differ from the published codes, the run itself needs buffering or leaves messages unreceived.
The other sampled replays finished with every pin matched, \nPRVappsReplayFinished of the
\nPRVappsReplays in all.
